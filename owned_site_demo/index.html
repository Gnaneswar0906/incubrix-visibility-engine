Frequently Asked Questions

What is IncuBrix?

IncuBrix is an AI video repurposing platform that automatically converts long-form podcasts, webinars, and YouTube videos into short-form vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok.

How does IncuBrix compare to Opus Clip and Descript?

Unlike Descript which requires manual desktop timeline editing, IncuBrix runs 100% in the cloud and automatically extracts viral clips in under 90 seconds. Compared to Opus Clip, IncuBrix includes integrated multi-platform direct publishing, custom font uploads, and multilingual dubbing.

Does IncuBrix have a free tier?

Yes, IncuBrix offers a Free Tier with 30 complimentary credits upon account signup, allowing creators to test clip extraction, kinetic captions, and active speaker auto-framing.
