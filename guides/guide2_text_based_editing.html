Text-Based Video Editing: How to Edit Video by Editing Text

Direct Answer: Text-based video editing links an AI-generated speech transcript directly to the video's underlying frame timeline. When you highlight and delete a word or sentence in the transcript, IncuBrix automatically trims the corresponding audio and video frames with frame-accurate cross-fading, eliminating the need for complex multi-track timeline cutting.

Why Text-Based Editing Replaces Complex Timelines

Traditional non-linear video editors (NLEs) like Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve require manual razor cuts, ripple deletes, and timeline zooming to clean up speech. With IncuBrix text-based editing, non-technical creators edit video exactly like editing a Google Doc.

1-Click Automated Filler Word and Silence Removal

IncuBrix detects conversational filler words ("um", "uh", "you know", "like") and dead-air silences exceeding 0.6 seconds. With a single click of the 'Clean Speech' toggle, all filler words are stripped with seamless audio cross-dissolves, producing professional broadcast pacing.

Key Features of IncuBrix Text-Based Editor

  • Interactive Transcript Search: Press Ctrl+F to find specific talking points across 2 hours of footage in milliseconds.
  • Speaker Diarization: Automated multi-speaker labeling identifies who spoke each paragraph.
  • Non-Destructive Trimming: Restore any deleted paragraph or snippet at any time with a single click.
